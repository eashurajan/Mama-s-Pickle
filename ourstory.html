Deepa Lakshmi

Mama’s Pickle Founder & CEO

A Little Taste of Home, A Growing Tradition

Every great food tradition starts somewhere with a familiar taste, a family recipe, and the joy of sharing something delicious with the people around us. Mama's Pickles was built around that simple feeling. Inspired by my mom’s the flavours we grew up enjoying, we set out to bring the taste of homemade pickles to more homes, one jar at a time.

Growing Together, One Jar at a Time

From our first 500 customers in January 2025 to over 1,000 within three months, our growth has been powered by customer love and word-of-mouth recommendations.

From Neighbourhood Favourite to Local Store Shelves

As our community grew, we partnered with local retail stores in Coimbatore, making Mama's Pickles easier to find and enjoy.

Taking Our Flavours Across South India

From our neighbourhood beginnings, we've expanded our reach across Palakkad, Andhra Pradesh, and Bangalore, bringing our flavours to more homes.

More Than Pickles, It's a Taste of Home

Every jar carries our love for homemade flavours, shared memories, and good food. Thank you for being part of the Mama's Pickles journey.
